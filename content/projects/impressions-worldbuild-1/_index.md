# I. The Sundering

A long time ago the whole world was one large piece of land with a thriving cultures possessing advanced technology at war with each other, but in their hubris of trying to reach ever higher, they eventually caused an event that destroyed everything and lead to the land shattering apart into multiple small islands.

Their once mighty and proud cities now lay in ruins, their technology and machines resting rusted and forgotten, to the sands of time.
